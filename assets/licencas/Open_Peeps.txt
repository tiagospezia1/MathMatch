The portraits in assets/imagens/personagens/ were generated from the Open Peeps
parts using the DiceBear "open-peeps" package (https://www.dicebear.com/styles/open-peeps/,
design files CC0 1.0, code MIT) and then edited: the professor's beard was recolored
and a top hat was drawn for the villain.
